Privacy Policy for img2prompt

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Publisher: baha@bahasavage.com

1. Overview

img2prompt ("we", "us", or "the extension") provides image-to-prompt reverse-engineering tools for creators. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through strict data minimization policies.

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identity documents.
  • User-Initiated Image Data: When you deliberately paste an image into the extension and click "Submit", the image data is temporarily transmitted via encrypted HTTPS to our generation endpoint to generate a text prompt. The image is never used for training or shared with third parties.
  • Authentication Keys: Your license key is stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local and transmitted in request headers (x-api-key) strictly to verify remaining generation quotas.
  • Prompt History: Your prompt generation history and thumbnails are stored strictly on your local device via chrome.storage.local. We do not sync or upload your history to external servers.

3. Permissions Justifications

  • clipboardWrite: Used solely to allow you to copy generated prompts to your clipboard with a single click.
  • storage: Used solely to save your license key, active draft session, and last 20 generation history entries locally on your device.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not inject advertisements or run tracking analytics.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at: baha@bahasavage.com.
